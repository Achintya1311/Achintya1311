# Achintya Mishra

Equity research candidate combining data science and finance. BTech (Computer & IoT), Manipal University Jaipur.

**Site:** [achintya1311.github.io](https://achintya1311.github.io) — all of the below, one page.

- **Valuation:** [reverse-dcf-engine](https://github.com/Achintya1311/reverse-dcf-engine) solves for the growth, margin and reinvestment a share price implies, then tests them against history and peers. Paired with a [three-statement model](https://github.com/Achintya1311/three-statement-model) built from primary filings.
- **Pipeline:** [STOCKSTALKER](https://github.com/Achintya1311/STOCKSTALKER) is the front door to nine linked repos: valuation, three-statement model, Monte Carlo risk, an Indian factor model, options and sentiment. It reads every one of them into a single tear sheet for one stock.
- **Research:** CRAFT, a gate-driven ensemble validated leave-one-dataset-out across five datasets. Submitted to a conference in 2026.
- **Certifications:** NISM Series XV (Research Analyst) and NISM Series VIII (Equity Derivatives).

Each repo reports what failed as well as what worked. See the Limitations section of each README.

<sub>Educational research, not investment advice. Not a SEBI-registered research analyst.</sub>
